About us

Regional shipping with a personal point of contact

Silk Road Highway brings land freight, sea freight and vehicle transport together for customers shipping across the GCC and to Libya.

Built around the shipment

Every shipment has its own practical requirements. Our role is to bring those requirements into a workable plan.

A business moving stock, a customer importing a car and a supplier sending a commercial load need different arrangements. We start with the cargo and route, then coordinate the transport method, handling, documents and delivery requirements.

Our focus is shipping within the Gulf and to Libya: Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain and Libya. The agreed scope is set out before booking, with a clear point of contact for the arrangements along the way.

How we work

Practical planning, clear responsibilities and careful coordination at each stage.

Clear scope

Collection, transport, handling and destination services are identified before the shipment is confirmed.

Cargo-appropriate handling

Loading equipment, packaging and vehicle securing are considered for the goods being moved.

Direct communication

A straightforward way to discuss documents, collection readiness and delivery arrangements.

Across the GCC and Libya
